# Hi, I'm Hummad 👋

I'm a technical architect and engineering leader with 20+ years across
backend systems, full-stack delivery, and the business side of running
a technology organization. I've owned production architecture end to
end, but I've also sat on the other side of the table: setting technical
direction, managing budgets and vendors, and being accountable for
whether a platform actually made money, not just whether it ran cleanly.

My core depth is backend and systems architecture (.NET/C#, distributed
systems, cloud infrastructure), but I work comfortably across the full
stack (TypeScript, React, React Native) and I'm just as at home in a
conversation about technical trade-offs as I am in one about business
impact, staffing, or vendor risk.

I care more about how a system behaves under partial failure than how
clean it looks in a diagram. Most of what I've learned came from being
the person accountable when something went wrong, whether that meant
fixing the code or explaining the impact to the business.

## How I work

I write code, but I spend at least as much time deciding how a system
should be shaped: where the boundaries go, what happens when a
downstream dependency is slow or down, and what should page someone
versus what can wait until morning. I've also had to own the business
outcome of those decisions, not just the technical one, which changes
how you weigh a trade-off. The systems that hold up in production are
usually the ones where someone thought hard about failure modes and
cost before they shipped, not the ones that just looked good in review.
